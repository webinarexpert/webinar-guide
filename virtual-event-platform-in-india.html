Virtual Event Platform in India: Top Options Compared for 2026

TL;DR:

Choosing an event platform is not just about finding software that can stream sessions. Organisers may also need virtual venues, exhibitor spaces, networking, attendee management, and engagement tools. This comparison explores five options - Dreamcast, Airmeet, vFairs, Zoom Events, and RingCentral Events - and explains what each brings to different event formats.

Introduction

Imagine organising a virtual conference with keynote sessions, panel discussions, sponsor booths and hundreds of attendees. The sessions are ready, speakers are confirmed, and registrations are coming in. But how will attendees move between sessions? Where will exhibitors interact with visitors? How will participants connect?

These are the practical questions that should guide your platform selection. A video meeting tool may be sufficient for a small internal session, but a multi-track conference or virtual exhibition requires a more structured experience. The right virtual event platform should support the way your event operates, from the first attendee interaction to the final session.

India's virtual events market generated an estimated USD 4.9 billion in revenue in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 25.5 billion by 2033, according to Grand View Research. As organisations continue to explore digital event formats, understanding the differences between available solutions becomes increasingly important.

Here are five platforms to consider in 2026, along with the event experiences they are designed to support.

1. Dreamcast: For immersive virtual event experiences

Founded: 2009
Headquarters: Jaipur, India
G2 rating: 4.8/5

Some events need a digital venue where attendees can explore, interact and participate instead of simply watching a presentation. Dreamcast is designed for this kind of experience.

Its virtual event platform enables organisers to create customised virtual environments with lobbies, auditoriums and exhibitor booths. These spaces can be arranged around the event's purpose, whether it is a business conference, product launch, virtual exhibition, town hall or trade show.

The experience also extends beyond the venue design. Features such as AI matchmaking, a B2B meeting scheduler, attendee reactions, dynamic banners, and notifications help create opportunities for interaction. Integrations with WhatsApp, Zoom, and Google Meet can support communication and meeting workflows.

Another practical consideration is accessibility. Attendees can join through a browser without downloading an application, making participation more straightforward for different audiences.

Where it fits: Choose Dreamcast when your event requires a customised digital environment, multiple event spaces, and opportunities for attendee networking. It is particularly relevant for organisers looking for an immersive 3D Virtual Event Platform rather than a basic streaming interface.

2. Airmeet: When networking is central to the event

Founded: 2019
Headquarters: Bengaluru, India
G2 rating: 4.6/5

A conference can deliver excellent presentations and still leave attendees with very few meaningful connections. Airmeet approaches this challenge by placing interaction and networking at the centre of its event experience.

The platform offers virtual tables where participants can connect through video and chat. Organisers can also run live sessions, facilitate audience questions and create opportunities for attendees to interact with speakers and other participants.

This makes Airmeet relevant for professional communities, industry conferences, workshops, webinars and networking events. Its approach is particularly useful when the objective is to encourage conversations rather than simply maximise session attendance.

For organisers comparing the Best virtual event platform options, Airmeet is worth exploring when networking is a major part of the event programme.

Where it fits: Consider Airmeet for events where peer discussions, professional connections and audience participation are as important as the sessions themselves.

3. vFairs: For virtual fairs and exhibitions

Founded: 2016
Headquarters: Dallas, USA
G2 rating: 4.7/5

A virtual exhibition has different requirements from a regular webinar. Attendees may need to browse exhibitor booths, discover products, interact with representatives, and collect information from different participants. vFairs is built to support these more structured event journeys.

The platform provides virtual environments for exhibitions, career fairs, conferences and trade shows. Its broader event technology offering also includes registration, ticketing, exhibitor and speaker portals, lead capture, event marketing and reporting.

This combination can help organisers coordinate several parts of an event without treating the virtual venue as an isolated feature. Exhibitors can have dedicated spaces, while attendees can explore the event and engage with different participants.

Its venue-based approach also makes vFairs an option for businesses evaluating a 3D Virtual Event Platform for large exhibitions or multi-area events.

Where it fits: vFairs is worth shortlisting for virtual career fairs, recruitment events, trade shows and exhibitions where exhibitor discovery and lead generation are important.

4. Zoom Events: For familiar video-led experiences

Founded: 2011 (Zoom)
Headquarters: San Jose, USA
G2 rating: 4.5/5

Not every event needs a highly customised virtual venue. For many organisations, the priority is to host reliable sessions, manage registrations and give attendees a familiar way to participate. Zoom Events builds on Zoom's established video communication ecosystem.

The offering supports organised event experiences, including registration, event hubs, multi-session programmes and audience engagement. Tools such as Q&A, polls, chat and recordings can support interaction during webinars and online conferences.

Its familiarity can also be useful when speakers, employees or attendees already use Zoom regularly. This may reduce the effort required to introduce participants to a new interface.

As an online event platform, Zoom Events is particularly relevant when video delivery and structured webinar experiences are the main requirements. Organisations should still review its event customisation and networking capabilities against more immersive alternatives.

Where it fits: Consider Zoom Events for webinar programmes, training sessions, company meetings and conferences where video communication is the primary focus.

5. RingCentral Events: For branded virtual and hybrid programmes

Founded: 1999 (RingCentral)
Headquarters: Belmont, USA
G2 rating: 4.5/5

Some organisations run several types of events throughout the year and want a consistent branded experience across them. RingCentral Events supports this requirement through a platform designed for webinars, virtual conferences and hybrid events.

The platform provides different event areas, including stages, breakout spaces, expo areas and networking rooms. Organisers can use engagement tools such as live Q&A, polls and reactions to involve attendees during sessions.

It also includes event production and reporting capabilities. RingCentral highlights integrations, reusable content and analytics as part of its wider event offering. These features can be useful for teams that want to manage event delivery and continue using content after the live programme ends.

For businesses looking for a virtual event management platform that can support different event formats, RingCentral Events is worth evaluating.

Where it fits: It can suit marketing teams, businesses and associations organising branded webinars, customer events, virtual conferences and hybrid programmes.

Making the final choice!

Each platform in this comparison serves a different event requirement. Dreamcast focuses on customised virtual environments and networking. Airmeet places emphasis on attendee connections, while vFairs is particularly relevant for fairs and exhibitions. Zoom Events supports familiar video-led programmes, and RingCentral Events offers branded virtual and hybrid experiences.

There is no universal winner. The Best virtual event platform is the one that matches your event format, audience expectations, technical requirements, and budget. Shortlist solutions based on these factors, request demonstrations, and test the attendee journey before committing.

FAQs

1. What is the difference between a virtual event platform and a webinar tool?

A webinar tool hosts live sessions, while a virtual event platform can also support networking, registration, multiple sessions, and exhibitor spaces.

2. When should I choose a 3D virtual event platform?

Choose a 3D Virtual Event Platform when attendees need to explore virtual venues, visit booths, and interact with exhibitors.

3. Can an online event platform support large conferences?

Yes. An online event platform can support multi-session conferences, registration, and attendee engagement, depending on its capacity and features.

4. What makes a virtual event management platform suitable for businesses?

A virtual event management platform should simplify registration, attendee management, session coordination, reporting, and engagement.
